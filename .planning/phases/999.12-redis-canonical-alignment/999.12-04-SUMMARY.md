# Phase 999.12 Plan 04: Sender CacheModule → outbound/cache/ Migration Summary

Migrate sender's pre-existing `CacheModule.forRootAsync({ namespace: 'sender' })` from `bootstrap/health/health.module.ts` (where Phase 21 placed it transitively) to a new `outbound/cache/cache.module.ts` thin wrapper (`AppCacheModule`) that mirrors `AppPersistenceModule`. Namespace pulled from the catalog (`SERVICE.sender.id`) rather than a hardcoded literal. HealthModule loses its direct foundation `CacheModule` import and instead pulls `AppCacheModule` (Ring 4 → Ring 3 cross-ring). Establishes the canonical structural template that Plans 05-09 will mirror across the other 5 services.
