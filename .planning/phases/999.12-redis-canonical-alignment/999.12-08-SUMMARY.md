## Decisions Made

- **Mixed DI styles preserved verbatim** — class-based `RabbitMqHealthIndicator` stays class-based (no Symbol promotion in 999.12 scope per RESEARCH §S-5); only `REDIS_HEALTH` added as a Symbol-based DI inject (matching the existing `PUBLIC_BUCKET_HEALTH` Symbol-based pattern). Future phase will own the rabbitmq class→Symbol promotion.
- **REDIS_HEALTH inject placed as 4th arg** (last position in constructor) and **3rd readiness item** (last position in array) — additive ordering avoids touching existing inject/check positions, minimising review surface.
- **CACHE_CONFIG_PORT at notifier narrowPorts position 2** (after `LOGGING_CONFIG_PORT`, before `STORAGE_CORE_CONFIG_PORT`) — notifier has no `PERSISTENCE_CONFIG_PORT` (no PostgreSQL), so the sender canonical "Redis after Logging" rule lands at position 2 here vs position 3 in sender.
- **Root composition root left untouched** — `notifier.module.ts` already imports `HealthModule`; transitive resolution chain (`HealthModule.imports → AppCacheModule.exports → CacheModule.exports → [TerminusModule, CACHE_SERVICE, REDIS_HEALTH, REDIS_CLIENT]`) makes `REDIS_HEALTH` visible to the controller without root-level changes.
