## Decisions Made

1. **Strict replication from Wave 2 AuthClient template.** No per-upstream deviations — every difference was a mechanical substitution (proto namespace, class name, SERVICE.<id>, method list). This is exactly what PATTERNS.md entry 7 prescribes and what 999.7.2-02 pilot demonstrated.

2. **Absorb Wave 2 AuthClient prettier leftover as a separate `style(...)` commit.** Wave 2 merged AuthClient without running prettier on the new constructor, producing 2 prettier/prettier errors. Since this plan's success criteria include "Gateway lint green", the cleanup had to happen here. Split as a separate commit from Task 4 to keep Task 4's scope purely notifier-related and the cleanup auditable.

3. **Sibling-module typecheck failures during per-task verification are expected, not blocking.** Wave 2 changed `defineGrpcClient`'s build callback signature from 3-arg → 2-arg. Between Wave 2 merge and the completion of this plan, audience/parser/notifier modules still had 3-arg callbacks — that made their typecheck fail even before my Task 1 edit. Each task's path-scoped typecheck (errors only in `clients/<id>/`) stayed clean throughout. Full-gateway typecheck went green only after Task 4, which matches the plan's narrative in success_criteria ("gateway typecheck green after this plan").
